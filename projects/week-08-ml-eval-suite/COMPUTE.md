# Compute note

**Tier:** 🟢 local CPU · **Expected time:** under 2 minutes for tests and notebook · **Budget:** $0

The examples use small built-in scikit-learn datasets. No GPU, cloud provider, download, credentials, or external tracking service is needed. `n_jobs=1` is intentional: it keeps fold behavior reproducible and avoids oversubscribing a laptop during a lesson.
