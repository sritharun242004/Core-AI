# Outreach and behavioral preparation

No message is sent by this repository. Research current roles, obtain consent before sharing anyone's details, and personalize rather than mass-mail.

## Eight evidence-backed stories

Prepare one real story each for ambiguity, disagreement, failure, debugging, ownership, collaboration, prioritization and ethical judgment. Use situation, task, action, result and reflection. Separate your contribution from the team's; if no numerical result exists, describe verifiable qualitative evidence instead of inventing numbers.

## Recruiter-call rehearsal

In 60 seconds state current role, relevant project evidence, target role and why that team. Ask about interview surfaces, coding-assistance policy, location/timezone, accessibility needs and expected timeline. Do not assume public anecdotes describe the current loop.

## A concise outreach structure

Subject: the specific role or technical question.
Opening: one genuine reason this team's public work is relevant.
Evidence: one sentence about your artifact, a link and its measured/limited result.
Ask: a specific, low-pressure request such as whether the role fits your background.
Close: thank them; do not imply a referral, relationship or result you do not have.

Keep personal information out of public issue threads. One respectful follow-up after a reasonable interval is enough; no automated repeated outreach.

## Compensation and scheduling

Compare base, bonus, equity type/vesting, liquidity, tax jurisdiction and benefits. Public salary aggregators are noisy samples, not offers. RSUs and profit-participation units are not interchangeable cash. Get written terms and qualified advice for tax/legal questions. For London/European teams, confirm timezone and daylight-saving offsets for every calendar invitation; do not infer location flexibility from an old job post.

## Portfolio review

Choose capstone plus two strongest assignments. Each needs a reproducible command, tests, honest benchmark boundary, clear README, clean secrets scan and a short demo. Link evidence rather than claiming that finishing this curriculum guarantees an interview or job.
